OUR TEST-FREE POLICY

Pitzer takes a holistic approach to admissions, focusing on high school transcripts, recommendation letters, leadership roles, work experience, community involvement, and alignment with the College’s values. We're test-free, allowing you to showcase your unique talents and potential without the pressure of standardized tests.
