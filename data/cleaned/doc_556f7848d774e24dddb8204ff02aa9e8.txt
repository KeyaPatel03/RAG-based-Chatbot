LIBERAL STUDIES DEPARTMENT

THE DEPARTMENT

The Liberal Studies Department prepares students to think across disciplines and understand diverse perspectives, fostering a human-centered, problem-solving mindset. It offers a Liberal Studies degree for aspiring K-9 educators and careers in human services and other professions, as well as an Interdisciplinary General Education (IGE) pathway that fulfills GE requirements across multiple disciplines. Both programs emphasize hands-on learning through Poly X experiences, field trips, and work with children.
