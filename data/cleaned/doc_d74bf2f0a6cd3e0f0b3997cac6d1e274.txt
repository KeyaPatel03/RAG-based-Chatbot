PROGRAM DETAILS

The public relations major is an innovative program that offers in-depth study of
the field and emphasizes hands-on experience. Students create their own professional
portfolio, gain experience working in APU’s student-run agency, join the local chapter
of the Public Relations Student Society of America, develop global public relations
skills through international study-away trips, and become specialists in nonprofit,
entertainment, or sports PR.
