For anyone who has completed junior year in high school, it is never too early to begin an application.
There is no application fee, and we accept applications on a rolling basis.

Begin your application by choosing the campus you wish to apply for, California or New England.

Our application is deliberately broad so as to allow students greater latitude in expressing their academic interests and strengths, and to enable the Admissions Committee to evaluate an applicant’s potential for success in the program. We encourage applicants to complete their applications as soon as possible for the best possible chance of enrolling.

While the majority of incoming students apply during their senior year of high school, we welcome applications from students who have already begun college elsewhere, and each year a sizable number of such students enroll. All applicants use the same application.

Should you wish to apply to the other campus at any point in your application process, or even after admission, you may do so prior to June 30, provided that space is available. Applicants need only complete the application form for the appropriate campus; all other application materials will transfer. After the class has been filled, all other accepted students will be placed on a waiting list.
