WHAT MAKES THE MOUNT?

A COMMITMENT TO FINANCIAL AID.

100% of traditional undergraduate student receive financial aid. For the 2025-2026 academic year, the average financial aid offer is $50,052.

EDUCATING THE FUTURE SINCE 1925.

We were the first to offer a nursing program in California. Today, we offer more than 40 programs, including film, business, exercise science and psychology.

WOMEN’S COLLEGES MAKE A DIFFERENCE.

Graduates of women’s colleges are more likely to attain their PhD, achieve more in their careers and, ultimately, earn more.

INCLUSIVE EXCELLENCE IS FUNDAMENTAL.

88 percent of our undergraduate students are women of color. 62 percent of our undergraduate students are the first in their families to attend college. We’re committed to fostering a welcoming community.

YOUR NEW HOME AWAITS.

Since 1925, our Chalon campus has been the heart of our undergraduate programs. You’ll be surrounded by incredible and historical architecture. And, more importantly, a community of Unstoppable students. Come see it for yourself.
