WHAT DOES THE REGISTRAR'S OFFICE DO?

We assist students with registration, help maintain academic records, verify transcripts and assist with course catalog maintenance and class scheduling. The Office of the Registrar delivers accurate, prompt, courteous and consistent services to all students and constituents of UC Riverside. The
Registrar
takes a leadership role in management of registration, student academic records, course and classroom scheduling, classroom utilization, publications production, and data distribution.
