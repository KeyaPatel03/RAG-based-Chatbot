TO BE CONSIDERED FOR FINANCIAL AID, NEW AND CONTINUING STUDENTS MUST MEET ALL THE FOLLOWING REQUIREMENTS:

• Be a U.S. citizen or eligible noncitizen.
• Have a valid Social Security number.
• Be admitted to or enrolled at CDU.
• Have a high school or general education diploma.
• Not be in default on any student loan.
• No refund is owed for any grant.
• Make satisfactory academic progress as determined by CDU.
Some California residents who were born abroad may qualify for the CA Dream Act. For more information on the Dream Act, please visit the
California Student Aid Commission Website
.

COMPLETING THE FREE APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL STUDENT AID (FAFSA)

• Create a Federal Student Aid (FSA) ID . You do not need to create a new FSA ID if you have an existing FSA ID.
• Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) . The FAFSA is open for submissions each October 1st. The FAFSA considers income from two years before the application year, referred as Prior-Prior Year (PPY). Eligible students may use the IRS Data Retrieval Tool to import their tax information.
• Include Charles R. Drew University in your FAFSA school list. CDU’s FAFSA School Code is 013653.
• Sign your FAFSA using your FSA ID
• Once submitted, your FAFSA will be processed, and your Student Aid Index (SAI) will be calculated. CDU will use your SAI to determine your financial aid eligibility.

Borrow Smart:
When it comes to student loans, only accept the amount you need. You do not have to accept the entire loan amount offered to you. Loan repayment is a long-term commitment and obligation so plan wisely and budget accordingly. A repayment estimator can be found on the
Federal Student Aid website
.

If you accepted any loan(s) offered to you, you must complete the following steps in order to have loan funds released to you.

• Complete Entrance Counseling
• Complete the Master Promissory Note (MPN)
• Complete a credit check (Parent PLUS and Graduate PLUS loan borrowers only)
Parent PLUS and Graduate PLUS loans are contingent upon credit approval. Once completed, a credit approval will be valid for six months. A new credit check must be completed to receive any awarded or undisbursed PLUS loan funds after the six-month period.

A student may still borrow a Direct PLUS Loan if they obtain an endorser with positive credit history. An endorser is someone who agrees to repay a Direct PLUS Loan if the borrower doesn’t repay the loan, much like a cosigner does for other types of loans.

Ensure all documents are completed
in the following order. A PLUS loan will not be disbursed if any of the documents are missing.

• Graduate PLUS Loan Application to be completed by the student.
• Direct PLUS Loan Endorser Addendum to the Master Promissory Note to be completed by the endorser. You will need to provide the endorser with an endorser code or an award identification number . The endorser code may be emailed to you from the U.S. Department of Education. Otherwise, it can be found on your Direct PLUS Loan application.
• Complete PLUS Credit Counseling.
• Complete new Graduate PLUS Master Promissory Note (student).
You may access your student billing/account information through the myCDU portal. You may also make payments and/or set up a payment plan through the portal. Please contact the Office of Student Finance for any billing/student account related questions.

YOU
must compare your financial aid awards with the amount due on your bill. If the total of your awards is:

• More than the amount due, you are not required to make a payment. When your financial aid is disbursed it will pay off the amount due.
• Less than the amount due, resulting in a remaining balance, you must pay this balance by the deadline. Please contact the Office of Student Finance for questions regarding payment.
Please note,
payment plans are designed to provide payment flexibility during the term of the semester and cannot exceed installments longer than the semester.
