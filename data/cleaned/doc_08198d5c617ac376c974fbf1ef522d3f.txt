STEPS TO FINANCIAL AID

• Be admitted to NDNU
• Complete the FAFSA or CADAA with school code 001179
• Submit any requested documents
• You will be emailed your Finanial Aid Offer and it will be viewable on the financial
 aid Net Partner portal.
• Receive your funds

Financial Aid disbursements are credited to your student account three weeks into
the semester
