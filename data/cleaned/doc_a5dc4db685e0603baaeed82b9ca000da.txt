BFA IN FASHION DESIGN

IN OTIS'S TOP-RANKED FASHION DESIGN PROGRAM, STUDENTS LEARN HOW TO CREATE AND CONSTRUCT
 GARMENTS UNDER LEADING INDUSTRY PROFESSIONALS, CALLED MENTORS, AND FACULTY.

For decades, the Otis College BFA in Fashion Design has prepared students for a seamless
transition into the global fashion industry. The program has successfully prepared
creative professionals for the rigors of the fashion industry. The program’s reputation
is based on quality, relevance, and innovation. This has been accomplished in part
by developing an extensive and comprehensive networking component into the program.

FASHION DESIGN AT OTIS

With abundant hands-on guidance and access to a wealth of materials, our students
never compete for attention or resources. The Fashion Design program differentiates
itself with a singular emphasis on developing capable, innovative, and professional
fashion designers with a comprehensive understanding of consumer markets, from moderate
to high-end. To prepare our graduates for meaningful careers, we emphasize the technical,
interpersonal, creative, and collaborative skills that matter most in the field. Junior
and Senior mentorships challenge students to design with an eye toward sustainability,
uniting creativity with technology and innovation.

WHAT YOU’LL LEARN

The cornerstone of our Fashion Design curriculum is providing students the opportunity
to work directly with successful industry professionals.
These mentors help our students gain fresh, industry-focused perspectives on design.

Our core curriculum complements this hands-on approach by developing capable, professional
fashion designers through the following pillars:

• Taking direction
• Clothing construction
• Drawing by hand
• Digital skills
Program Curriculum

OUR COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY

Sustainability is core to our approach to fashion. Every mentor project touches on
this concept in some way. Some of our brand partners also provide fabrics or recycled
materials that are sustainably utilized in projects.

OTIS FASHION SHOWS

Our students are known for producing high-caliber work. Both the junior and the senior
classes present their designs at the annual fashion show that is part of the O-Launch
exhibition weekend. Top senior student work is also showcased at Otis Atelier, an
annual fashion benefit that supports scholarships for first-generation Otis College
students.

CAREER POSSIBILITIES

Otis alumni enter the fashion industry as assistant designers, associate designers,
illustrators, costume designers, textile designers, accessory designers, and product
designers.

Though some graduates opt to launch their own lines, approximately 86% Fashion Design
graduates are employed as assistant fashion designers within three months of earning
their BFA degree.

Careers and Internships

EMBRACING TECHNOLOGY

We consider our Fashion Design department as a think tank for industry innovation.
Through partnerships with industry leaders we explore and embrace new technology as
quickly as it becomes available.

MENTORS

We place professional designers alongside Fashion Design students. This offers a glimpse
of the future of fashion design from those who are creating it. The fashion department’s
philosophy fosters collaborations with industry professionals serving as Mentors.
These Mentors are invited into the classrooms, giving Juniors and Seniors an authentic
current design challenge, sharing their expertise and guiding students through the
design process.
