BFA IN PRODUCT DESIGN

OTIS COLLEGE’S BFA PRODUCT DESIGN PROGRAM PROVIDES A UNIQUE ENVIRONMENT WHERE CREATIVITY
 AND INNOVATION INTERSECT.

In our BFA Product Design program, you will explore a wide range of projects that
will develop your individual voice, build your skills as a designer, and lead you
to a rewarding career. Within our multidisciplinary studio environment, we foster
curiosity and skills that are key to creating products and experiences in both physical
and digital form that people interact with every day.

WHY STUDY PRODUCT DESIGN AT OTIS

At Otis College, we want you to feel limitless in what you can create. Our BFA Product
Design program provides the tools and resources to integrate your voice, vision, and
interests into a life of design. Work with wood, metal, fabric, fiber, leather, rubber,
ceramic, glass, plastics, synthetics, and other materials to create beautiful and
functional objects that improve everyday lives.

We help you discover your unique strengths and passions. Our curriculum introduces
you to a diverse set of tools and skills, allowing you to develop a distinct design
voice while mastering the core processes of product design and development. Through
personalized guidance, we equip you with the expertise and strategic thinking needed
to build a sustainable, adaptable career in design.
The program is also STEM-designated, allowing international students to apply for
the OPT extension and work in the U.S. for up to three years after graduation.

WHAT YOU’LL LEARN

The Otis College BFA in Product Design is a unique blend of art, design, business,
and entrepreneurship. Our multidisciplinary approach integrates 2D, 3D, and 4D digital
and physical skills with hands-on studio projects and professional opportunities,
equipping you to create products that enhance the human experience and drive cultural
innovation.

CAREER POSSIBILITIES

Otis College’s Product Design program prepares students to thrive in today’s fast-changing
industries. With an emphasis on adaptability, strong 2D and 3D design skills, manufacturing
knowledge, craftsmanship, and real-world problem solving, our multidisciplinary education
opens doors to a wide range of careers in creative and technical fields.

Our graduates are hired by global companies, launch their own businesses, and work
as independent contractors across many creative industries. Some make their mark in
furniture, consumer electronics, or experience-driven design. Others pursue opportunities
in UI/UX and digital product design, or enter the fashion world, where a number have
applied their entrepreneurial spirit and bold ideas to create successful apparel brands.

Our personalized approach encourages students to explore a range of design methods,
materials, and industries, helping them discover the path that fits them best. Otis
Product Design graduates begin their careers with titles like designer, junior designer,
associate designer, and even creative director, working across industries including:

RECENT EMPLOYERS

• Furniture and Home Décor
• Consumer Electronics
• Fashion and Fashion Accessories
• Footwear and Workwear
• App and UX/UI Design

• Medical Devices
• Recreation, Sports Equipment, and more
Whether your goal is to work for a major brand or start something of your own, Otis
Product Design helps you gain the skills, tools, confidence, and creative vision needed
to shape the future of design while building a meaningful, sustainable career.
