Allyson Felix, one of USC’s most celebrated alumni, stands outside the USC Allyson Felix Field, renamed in her honor April 10, 2023. Felix has been a hero both on and off the track, earning the most Olympic medals of any track athlete in U.S. history. She also received an honorary Doctor of Humane Letters degree from USC  in 2022. Photo by Gus Ruelas.
