THERE ARE SEVERAL WAYS TO GET IN TOUCH WITH USC ADMISSION. FIND WHAT YOU NEED BELOW.

ASK THE OFFICE OF ADMISSION

The
askUSC
portal will help you find answers to our most commonly asked questions — and even some you may not have thought about. If you can’t find your answers in the portal you can email the Office of Admission at
askUSC
.

FIND YOUR ADMISSION COUNSELOR

Search for the
USC Admission Counselor
who works with your school. They will serve as your primary point of contact in the Office of Undergraduate Admission.

LEARN MORE ABOUT USC

Submit a
Request for Information
to get on our email list.

GET YOUR PERSONALIZED DISCOVER USC SITE

Receive information tailored specifically to you: Register online and we’ll send you a link to your own
personalized Discover USC site
.

VISIT YOUR USC APPLICANT PORTAL

Once you’ve applied or been admitted to USC, check your
USC Applicant Portal
on a regular basis to make sure you have submitted all needed materials and to access a range of helpful resources.

Applicant Portal/Online Status Check

Once you have applied to USC, you will receive a confirmation email from our office with instructions on how to set up your account in the
USC Applicant Portal
.

Check your portal on a regular basis to make sure you have submitted all needed material and to access a range of helpful resources.

FOLLOW-UP APPLICATION MATERIALS

Students are encouraged to submit their transcripts electronically. Information about sending e-transcripts can be found in the
askUSC knowledgebase
.

Letters of recommendation that cannot be submitted through the Common App can be emailed to
admlors@usc.edu
.

Materials that cannot be submitted electronically should be mailed to the following address:
USC Office of Admission
Undergraduate
University Park Campus
Los Angeles, CA 90089-0911
