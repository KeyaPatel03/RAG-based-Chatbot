STATE AUTHORIZATION FOR ONLINE PROGRAMS

The Department of Education requires that institutions offering online or distance
learning programs outside of their home states must seek authorization to operate
from regulatory bodies in those states. Each state has a different set of requirements
when it comes to allowing out-of-state colleges and universities to offer online degree
programs to its residents.
