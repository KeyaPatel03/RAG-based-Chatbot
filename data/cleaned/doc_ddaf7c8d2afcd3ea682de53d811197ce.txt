PROGRAM DETAILS

History is the study of the human record of the human past. As an academic discipline,
history is comprehensive insofar as it records and explains the development, causes,
and effects over time of all other disciplines. Thoughtful study of the past provides
a necessary perspective for making judgments in the present. It instructs the student
in human possibilities and limits. Course offerings include studies in American, European,
and world history, as well as focused studies of various historical themes and different
regions.
