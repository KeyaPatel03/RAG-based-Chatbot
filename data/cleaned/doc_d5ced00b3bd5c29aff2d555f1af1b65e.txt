TUITION PAYMENT PLANS

To make tuition more manageable, we offer interest-free payment plans designed to provide flexibility while ensuring students can keep up with their educational expenses. Payment plans allow you to spread the cost of tuition across multiple months while remaining enrolled.

MONTHLY INSTALLMENTS

Tuition can be divided into manageable monthly payments, helping you stay on track without large upfront costs.

FLEXIBLE SCHEDULING

Plans are structured around academic terms, making it easy to align your payments with your course schedule.

NO INTEREST CHARGES

Unlike traditional loans, these plans allow you to pay over time without incurring any interest or additional fees.

SIMPLE ENROLLMENT PROCESS

Setting up a payment plan is quick and easy, so you can focus on your studies instead of financial paperwork.

VIEW TUITION PLAN DETAILS

Our tuition plans are designed to make paying for your education straightforward and manageable. Whether you’re enrolled in a standard semester-based program or the Juris Doctor Executive Track, flexible installment options allow you to spread tuition costs over time without added interest.

STANDARD SEMESTER-BASED PLAN

• Students pay 30% of the semester’s tuition at the start of the term.
• The remaining balance is divided into three equal monthly payments.
• Tuition for the current semester must be fully paid before enrolling in the next semester.

JURIS DOCTOR EXECUTIVE TRACK PLAN

• This program follows an academic year structure instead of semesters.
• Students pay 30% of the academic year’s tuition upfront.
• The remaining balance is spread across 12 equal monthly payments during the academic year.

EMPLOYER TUITION REIMBURSEMENT

Many employers offer tuition assistance or reimbursement programs to support continuing education. If your employer provides tuition benefits:

• Pay your tuition directly to WHTU.
• Request the required documentation from our financial aid office.
• Submit the documentation to your employer for approval.
• Receive reimbursement from your employer based on their policy.
We will assist in providing necessary verification or documentation for employer tuition assistance programs.

CANCELLATION AND REFUND POLICY

William Howard Taft University maintains a fair and equitable tuition refund policy in compliance with regulatory requirements. This policy may change to reflect legislative or accrediting updates.

Each 8-week session is independent for refund purposes. If a student cancels enrollment within the first seven days, they receive a full refund. After that, withdrawals or dismissals qualify for a prorated refund:

No refunds are issued after the sixth week, and completed courses are non-refundable. For example, a student withdrawing on Day 25, having paid $1,000, would receive $400 (40%).

Refunds are based on the withdrawal notification date. Fees are non-refundable unless stated otherwise. Refunds are processed within 30 days.

Dismissals take effect on the date determined by Administration or when a student fails to meet academic standards. Students with tuition loans are responsible for full repayment, including interest. Some lenders require refunds to be issued directly, and students authorize the University to comply.
