BFA IN ANIMATION

WHILE EARNING A BFA IN ANIMATION AT OTIS COLLEGE, STUDENTS BRING CHARACTERS AND STORIES
 TO LIFE THROUGH AN IMMERSIVE AND RIGOROUS STUDIO PROGRAM.

The interdisciplinary BFA in Animation program at Otis College develops innovative
and adaptive storytellers. By helping students synergize creative skills, soft skills,
and production techniques, we equip graduates for inspiring work as 3D modelers, animators,
creative directors, visual effects artists, and more. Additionally, this degree is
STEM-designated, offering a unique blend of technical and creative training that enhances
career prospects in both artistic and technology-driven fields.

WHY STUDY ANIMATION AT OTIS?

For more than 25 years, Otis College’s Digital Media education has grown to meet the
evolving needs and opportunities of the animation industry. We now offer two distinct
programs: Animation and Game and Entertainment Design. Animation focuses on specific
tools and applications while delivering a core curriculum that includes rendering,
modeling, mathematics, character design, and complex graphics. The result is an incredibly
well-rounded education that prepares students to work in fields as varied as entertainment,
education, and the military.

OTIS COLLEGE NAMED A TOON BOOM CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE

Otis College of Art and Design has been recognized as a
Toon Boom Centre of Excellence
, highlighting our commitment to industry-standard animation education and creative
innovation. As part of this designation, Otis students and faculty receive access
to
Toon Boom Harmony and Storyboard Pro
, including complimentary annual desktop subscriptions available through graduation.
These professional tools are widely used across film, television, and digital media
production.

This accreditation ensures Otis students are learning with the same software trusted
by leading animation studios worldwide, preparing them for success in today’s animation
industry.

AREAS OF EMPHASIS

Animation includes multiple creative paths. 2D uses hand-drawn or digital imagery
for stylized, frame-by-frame motion, while 3D uses computer-generated models for depth
and realism. Storytelling drives narrative and emotion, and motion design uses animated
graphics like typography and branding to communicate ideas.
