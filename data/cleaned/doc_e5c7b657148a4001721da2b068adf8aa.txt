POLITICAL SCIENCE AND INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

Making the decision to take on a major or minor in
Political Science
and/or
International Relations
will allow students to learn about and explore the forces and institutions that govern the world in which we live. At LMU, students of Political Science and International Relations delve into an array of concepts such as national and foreign policy, political theory, and the challenges of creating a just and secure global society. In addition, students will get to learn from with award-winning
faculty
, present their
research
at academic conferences,
study abroad
through global immersion courses, and pursue
internships
that prepare them for meaningful careers.
