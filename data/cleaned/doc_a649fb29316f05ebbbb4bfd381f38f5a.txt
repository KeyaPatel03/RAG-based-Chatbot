ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE

The Department of Computer Science at University of Redlands focuses on the principles and practices of designing, analyzing, and developing software systems. Students build a strong foundation in software design and implementation, computation, and algorithms, and may explore advanced topics such as data analytics, artificial intelligence and neural networks, mobile application development, and software engineering.
