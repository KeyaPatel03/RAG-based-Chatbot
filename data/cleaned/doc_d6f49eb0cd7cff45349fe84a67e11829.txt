ANIMATION MINOR

INTRODUCTION

15 UNITS

The Animation Minor at LCAD is a comprehensive 15-unit program designed to expand students’ creative horizons and career opportunities through mastery of animation fundamentals. Students can choose from two curricular tracks, Character Animation and Experimental Animation. This minor provides a well-rounded foundation in multiple animation mediums, from traditional hand-drawn techniques, 3D computer-generated animation or experimental approaches; preparing students for diverse roles in the entertainment industry.

CAREER APPLICATIONS

This minor serves as a valuable complement to any major, broadening career options and providing students with versatile skills applicable across the entertainment industry. Whether pursuing traditional animation careers or integrating animation skills into other creative fields, students gain practical experience and technical proficiency that enhances their professional marketability.
The combination of technical training, artistic development, and hands-on production experience ensures graduates are well-prepared to contribute to the dynamic and evolving field of animation.
