When you study art and design at La Sierra University, you will:

• Take classes from professional artists who are producing and exhibiting their art regularly throughout Riverside and Los Angeles.
• Enjoy trips to the Museum of Contemporary Art, the Getty Museum, and local art exhibits in Pasadena, Long Beach, and other locations.
• Benefit from La Sierra’s partnership with design companies willing to examine and critique undergraduate work.
• Showcase your art in the Brandstater Gallery, an on-campus venue reserved for students and faculty in the program.
• Join an on-campus art club where you can meet students with similar interests who enjoy trips, documentaries, and discussion of the latest issues in the field of art.
The Art+Design department at La Sierra University Programs provide students with the tools to effectively communicate with diverse audiences, breaking through barriers through the power of creative expression. Our comprehensive curriculum not only develops technical skills but also fosters critical thinking, problem-solving, and creativity - all highly valued attributes in today's professional world. At La Sierra University, our students develop a keen eye for aesthetics and become intuitive, self-motivated individuals who closely observe their visual environment. As Art, Fine Arts, Graphic Design, or  Photography majors, students establish a strong foundation in color theory, visual design principles, photography, and the use of both traditional and digital media. Upon graduation, our students are equipped for successful careers, advanced education, and the ability to make meaningful contributions to society. Join us at La Sierra University and unlock your artistic potential in a supportive and inspiring environment.

Come and join the Art+Design department in our various engaging activities. Whether you enjoy the outdoors, artists' talks, or creative get-togethers with good food and movies, we always have something going on.

Our field trips allow you to be thoroughly inspired and to recharge those creative batteries. From trips to the beach and hikes in awe-inspiring nature to museum and gallery visits, we always try to connect to the world in which we create.

Whether you are an Art major or not, you are always welcome to join our artists' talks, where amazing artists and seasoned creatives give you insights into their work and life. These talks allow you to learn from their experiences, find inspiration, learn how to avoid pitfalls, ask your unanswered questions, connect, and collaborate.

Our creative community invites you hang out with us at our relaxed get-togethers where we cook, enjoy good food, and watch thought-provoking, artsy, or just plain fun movies. No matter the activity, everyone is welcome!
