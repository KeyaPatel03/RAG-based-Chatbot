SUMMARY OF LEARNING OBJECTIVES FOR THE BACHELOR’S IN BUSINESS MANAGEMENT

SDCC’s business degree curriculum prepares students academically and spiritually to meet the needs of a complex and competitive business world.

The bachelor’s in business management program aims to help graduates:

• Communicate clearly and effectively in a business setting.
• Develop creative critical-thinking skills and practical problem-solving abilities.
• Demonstrate an informed, global perspective and cultural awareness.
• Successfully collaborate with human resources and other departments within the organization.
• Embrace ethical business practices and a faith-integrated worldview.

ONLINE BUSINESS MANAGEMENT DEGREE CURRICULUM

Degree candidates must complete 123 credit hours of online coursework or campus-based evening classes to earn a BS in Business Management from San Diego Christian College. Course descriptions and other relevant information can be found in SDCC’s
2022-2023 Online Course Catalog.

Business Management majors may choose to pursue an emphasis or minor in a related discipline. Refer to the Online Program Emphasis and Minor Options page for details and course listings.

CAREER OPPORTUNITIES:

Graduates who earn a BS in Business Management from San Diego Christian College can leverage their degree to secure a corporate job or promotion or start their own company. Nonprofit organizations, educational institutions, and government agencies also contribute to the ongoing demand for qualified business professionals.

The Business Management degree program can prepare you for such roles as:

• Entrepreneur/Business Owner
• Office Manager
• Sales Manager
• Staff Supervisor
• Marketing Manager
• Operations Manager
• Human Resources Generalist
