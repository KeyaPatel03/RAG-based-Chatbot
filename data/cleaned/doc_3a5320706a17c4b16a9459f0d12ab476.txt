WELCOME TO THE CAL POLY ENGLISH DEPARTMENT

The Cal Poly English Department provides an inclusive learning environment to promote the study of language, literatures, and rhetorics in diverse contexts. We currently offer both a B.A. and M.A. in English as well as an English Minor. There is also an opportunity to pursue a minor in Linguistics and a certificate in Technical Professional Communication (TPC) or in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (TESOL).

We are committed to fostering a respect for difference within our curriculum, our scholarship, and our community. Our undergraduate and graduate students learn to read carefully and deeply; to think critically and creatively; to write clearly, persuasively, and ethically; and to understand how power structures and cultural practices shape the production and reception of texts. Our faculty offer courses in literature, creative writing, composition and rhetoric, technical and professional communication, linguistics, film, English education, and theory and criticism. Our interdisciplinary approach provides a rich foundation in English Studies for our majors, our graduate students, and General Education students.
