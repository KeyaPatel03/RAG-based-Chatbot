RN TO BSN ONLINE PROGRAM

The RN to Bachelor of Science in Nursing degree completion program offers a nontraditional approach for Registered Nurses with associate’s degrees in nursing (ADNs) to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Nursing degree in as few as three (3) semesters. This program is available fully online.

The program is designed so that RNs can work while continuing their nursing education. The program is designed so that RNs can work while continuing their nursing education. There is an in-person clinical component in semesters two and three. Preceptorships will be held during the week and arranged individually to meet students’ needs. All general study requirements can be completed through the Weekend/Evening & Online College.

ACCELERATED BS IN NURSING

The Accelerated Bachelor of Science in Nursing program offers the BSN program to students who already hold a bachelor's degree. The program is completed within one year, in three consecutive semesters; it is a rigorous program of study.

LVN TO RN

A non-degree registered nurse option is available for applicants licensed as LVNs in California. After completing 26 units of prescribed courses, the student is eligible to take the California examination for RN licensure
