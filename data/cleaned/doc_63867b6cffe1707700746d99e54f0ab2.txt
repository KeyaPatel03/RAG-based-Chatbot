INTERNATIONAL GRADUATE SCHOLARSHIPS

Golden Gate University welcomes students from all over the world and offers scholarships for all qualified international students who are attending our San Francisco campus in a master’s degree program in our School of Business, School of Accounting and Braden School of Tax. Admitted students that have not previously attended Golden Gate University and satisfy the admissions criteria will be automatically considered for an International Student Scholarship or Grant through the admission process. There is no application required for these scholarships.

Note: Students must be an international student who entered the United States using an F Visa. Students studying on a J visa are not eligible for these scholarships or grants.

INTERNATIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS

INTERNATIONAL PRESIDENT’S SCHOLARSHIP – $15,000

• The International President’s Scholarship is a partial-tuition scholarship that awards students a total of $15,000 to be divided evenly over the required units to complete their degree.
• Students are eligible to continue receiving this tuition scholarship as long as they continue to make academic progress to complete their program.
• This award may not be combined with any other GGU grant, scholarship nor specially priced programs or courses.

INTERNATIONAL WELCOME GRANT – $2,000

• The International Welcome Grant is a one-time award in the amount of $2,000 to be applied based on full-time enrollment during a student’s first term.
