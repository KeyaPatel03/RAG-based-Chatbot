Composition Master of Music

WRITE THE MUSIC YOU DREAMED OF.

Write the music you dreamed of.

The composition department both honors tradition and encourages innovation. Our four versatile faculty members closely mentor students while maintaining high-profile composing careers. We strongly emphasize interdepartmental collaboration, matching composers with performers, as well as offer frequent concerts and readings of student works.  You'll be able to take advantage of the extraordinarily rich musical life of San Francisco, home to some of the most groundbreaking musical activity of the past half-century.

Opportunities for composers include: the Pankonin Art Song Commission of $3,000; Highsmith Orchestra Competition which includes a performance by the SFCM Orchestra of the winning work, and an hour recording session for the runner up; Biennial Choral and Art Song Composition Competitions with $1,000 in prizes; annual concert of student string quartet movements with SFCM’s quartet in residence, the Namburg-winning Telegraph String Quartet; four hours of orchestra readings annually.

Award-winning David Conte is chair of the composition department and the composer of more than two hundred works.

Every year, SFCM hosts the Highsmith Competition, an internal composition contest that grants the winner a performance of the submitted work by the Conservatory Orchestra.

At SFCM composers find their voice and enter the world with the same confidence they bring to their compositions.

INDUSTRY ALLIANCES TO SUPPORT STUDENTS AND ARTISTS

Industry Alliances to Support Students and Artists

In recent years, SFCM acquired two leading artist management companies and an iconic record label to reimagine how elements of the music industry can better integrate to advance the human experience through music. For students, that means more opportunities to interact with touring artists and industry experts–including professional development workshops, side-by-side performances, and enhanced masterclasses.
