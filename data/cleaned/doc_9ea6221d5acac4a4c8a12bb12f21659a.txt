ECONOMICS

In the Department of Economics, we aim to cultivate a space that supports students as they develop and sharpen their skills of inquiry, with the goal of fostering a lifelong commitment to learning and serving one’s community. Our department has a strong commitment to increasing the diversity of the economics profession as well as using economics to produce a more just and equitable world. Students interested in a wide range of subjects such as financial systems, health care, data science, and sustainability issues will find other students and faculty who share their passions.

ABOUT

Economics at Redlands operates from a pluralist, interdisciplinary perspective to explore the ways in which humans work together and organize themselves to sustain and enhance material well-being. We offer three distinct degree programs in economics and one program in data science.

ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

• Bachelor of Arts in Data Science
• Bachelor of Arts in Economics
• Bachelor of Science in Financial Economics
• Bachelor of Science in Economics
