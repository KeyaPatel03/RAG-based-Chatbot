The U.S. Department of Education is updating the FAFSA system starting April 26, 2026. The change will temporarily affect all 2026–27 FAFSA applications that are submitted or updated on or after that date. This is not a Chico State-specific issue, but is affecting all CSU campuses as well as other institutions.

For FAFSA applicants, we will resume processing your aid as soon as we can. CADAA applicants will not be affected. Please check this website for updates.
